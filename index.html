Dune it: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Dune it app ("Dune it", "we") collects, why, who it is shared with, and your rights. Dune it is currently a test version: no payments are made through the app, and features may change.

The data controller is Dune Global Oy, Business ID 3530908-3, Höyrykatu 2 A 70, 00520 Helsinki, Finland.

Contact for privacy questions: duneit.support@gmail.com

Information created when you use the app

  • When you last read each chat, to show the orange "unread" markers, and which chats you deleted for yourself.
  • Account status, for example whether an account is suspended after a report.
  • Your phone's push address (a "push token"), if you allow notifications, so we can send you notifications about new offers and messages. It is removed when you log out or delete your account.
  • Sign-in records: our login provider keeps a security log of sign-ins and active sessions, including the time, your device's IP address and the type of device or app used.
  • Technical data handled by our service providers when the app connects to them, such as your device's IP address and the time of the request. We do not use it to track you.

Stored only on your phone

Your login session, your app settings (light/dark mode, language, showing example tasks), and small notes such as which pop-ups you have already seen. These stay on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Your location (only if you allow it)

If you allow location, the app uses your phone's location only on your phone: to show where you are on the map and how far tasks are from you. Your location is never sent to us or saved in our database. You can turn it off any time in your phone's settings; the app then measures distances from the neighbourhood in your profile. On Android, the map and your position dot are drawn by Google Maps, so Google's privacy policy also applies to it.

What we do not collect

  • Your location: it stays on your phone (see above). Task locations are the centre point of the neighbourhood chosen, not an exact address.
  • Your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data.
  • Payment information. There are no payments in the test version.
  • Advertising or tracking data. Dune it has no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking.

3. Why we use your data (legal basis)

  • To provide the service you signed up for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)): your account, tasks, offers, chat and reviews.
  • Our legitimate interest in keeping Dune it safe (Art. 6(1)(f)): handling reports, blocking and suspending accounts, preventing misuse, and keeping conversations for a limited time after an account is deleted so that scams and disputes can still be checked.
  • Legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)), where the law requires us to keep or hand over information.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account and profile: as long as your account exists.
  • Tasks, offers, messages and reviews: as long as your account exists, or until deleted.
  • Deleting a chat in the app removes it from your Messages list only. The other person keeps their copy, and the messages stay stored so reports and disputes can be checked.
  • Reports: closed reports are deleted automatically 12 months after they were made. Open reports are kept until a moderator has handled them.
  • Sign-in records: active sessions end when you sign out or delete your account. The login provider's security log is kept for a limited time.

Deleting your account: in the app, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account. After you confirm with your password:

  • Deleted immediately: your login, your name and profile, reviews about you, your blocks, and the reports you made. Your unfinished tasks are cancelled, and jobs you were given go back to the people who posted them.
  • Kept without your name: messages you sent and reviews you wrote stay visible to the people involved, shown as from "Deleted user". This protects the people you dealt with, for example if a payment or a job went wrong.
  • Deleted automatically later: those conversations are deleted 12 months after their last message, and any tasks left from your account are removed after 12 months. Reviews you wrote stay as anonymous reviews, because they are part of the other person's rating.

Copies in our service providers' backups disappear as those backups expire automatically. You can also ask us by email to delete your data.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your data (access and portability),
  • correct wrong data (you can also edit your profile in the app),
  • delete your data (you can also do this yourself in the app),
  • restrict or object to certain uses.

Send requests to duneit.support@gmail.com. We answer within one month.

You also have the right to complain to the Finnish data protection authority, the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (Tietosuojavaltuutetun toimisto): tietosuoja.fi.

7. Age

Dune it is meant for people aged 15 or older.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database access rules make sure each person can only see what they are allowed to, for example chats only between the two people involved. Passwords are never stored in readable form. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the app changes. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app.
